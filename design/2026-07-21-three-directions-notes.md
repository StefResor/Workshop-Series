# Three homepage style directions — built for first call (2026-07-21)

Prototype: single self-contained HTML with A/B/C switcher, using only real content from the current site (services, method, workshop schedule, homepage fees $300/$150). Wordmark leads with "Stefanie Schumacher" per Mike's call; Relational Diplomacy is the practice line. Delivered to Mike as a Cowork artifact ("stef-design-directions") + chat file.

## A — "The Gallery"
Mendes Wood / Petzel bones: white ground, hairline rules, sticky topbar, grotesque type (Archivo). Vibrancy comes from three color-field gradient panels (orange / cobalt / green) standing in for artwork — services presented like works, workshops presented like an exhibitions index. Accent: International Klein Blue. Safest, most "gallery."

## B — "The Signal"
Martine Syms energy: saturated violet field + chartreuse accent, Fraunces serif display, IBM Plex Mono labels, animated marquee announcing the workshop series, editorial numbered service list, full-bleed chartreuse "From reactivity to the Wise Adult" statement section. Boldest personality; hero line "The fight is never about the dishes."

## C — "The Wise Adult"
Warm bone ground (#F3EFE7), monumental Archivo Black uppercase type with outline treatment, single electric orange accent, marquee band of the five method words, brutal-but-warm cards and rules. Hero: "Say the hard thing skillfully." Confident/direct — matches her "structured, direct" positioning most literally.

## Shared decisions across all three
- Typographic wordmark (no logo) — consistent with her references; supports the font-based-brand path.
- No stock photography anywhere — vibrancy via color/type instead (also solves the no-assets problem and avoids the Gottman-style happy-couples look).
- Workshops rendered as a first-class content list (the future events feed), with dates + register affordances.
- Corrected/canonical homepage fees used ($300/75, $150/50); Bookings-page conflicts ignored pending Stef's answer.
