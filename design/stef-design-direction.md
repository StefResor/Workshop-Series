# Stef's design direction (assembled from her emails, 2026-07-21)

## Who Stef is / current site

Stefanie Schumacher — licensed psychotherapist (MS, LPC, EMDR-certified), private practice since 2015. Practice = "Relational Diplomacy": structured, direct couples + individual therapy for high-level professionals (tech/aerospace execs). Fees on site: $300/75-min couples, $150/50-min individual. Also runs a workshop series.

**Current site (stefanie-schumacher.com) is built on Wix.** Minimalist, marble textures, clinical-but-accessible tone. She has **Wix premium prepaid for ~11 more months but is explicitly open to switching platforms.**

## What she WANTS — "almost like an artist's site: color, vibrancy, visual enticement"

1. https://mendeswooddm.com/artists/125-neil-beloufa/ — gallery artist page: white space, tabbed sections, artwork grid, imagery-led.
2. https://www.petzel.com/artists/seth-price — gallery artist page: institutional minimalism, installation photography forward.
3. https://www.martinesy.ms/ — personal artist site: single-scroll, text-forward, personality-driven, playful touches. Most "personal" of the set.
4. https://www.mai.art/ — Marina Abramović Institute. **LEAST FAV**: the most austere, gray, institution-scale one.

Also sent 3 Wix templates (likely chosen for layout/feel, not category — worth confirming what she likes in each):
- wh-1432 "Electrician (Modern)" — sleek modern services layout
- wh-1375 "New Album Landing Page (Empowering)" — bold single-purpose promo page (musician)
- 5041 "Ice Skating School (Modern)" — clean classes/programs layout (maps to her workshop series)

## What she does NOT want — the standard therapist/relationship-guru look

- https://terryreal.com/ — thought-leader hub: headshot + book covers, training/courses/directory
- https://www.estherperel.com/ — premium-warm personal brand, multiple product entry points
- https://www.gottman.com/ — coral-accented "relationship science" platform, happy-couples stock photography
- https://www.ourritual.com — startup-y teletherapy product site, icons + "Get started" CTAs

**Anti-pattern summary:** warm-neutral palettes, smiling-couples photography, credential-forward authority signaling, guru personal-brand merchandising, SaaS-style CTA funnels.

**Pattern summary of what she likes:** curated, imagery-led, gallery-like presentation with real color and personality — her practice presented like a body of work rather than a service funnel. Least-fav (mai.art) shows the minimalism must be warm/vibrant, not cold/gray.

## Stack implication

Portfolio-style structured content (works/offerings/workshops/press as collections) maps cleanly to Sanity + modern front end (e.g., Next.js/Astro). Her 11 months of prepaid Wix is sunk cost, not a constraint — she said so herself. Custom design freedom is also the strongest argument against staying in Wix templates: none of her reference sites are achievable as stock Wix templates.
